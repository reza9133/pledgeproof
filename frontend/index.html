Three guarantees

Paid per milestone

Each tranche is a fixed share of the raise, set before anyone pledges.

Judged by validators

No platform and no vote. Independent validators read the proof page and must agree.

Creator has skin in the game

A bond of at least 5% of the goal is locked and goes to backers if the creator fails.

From pledge to payout

Five steps, all enforced by the contract and judged by GenLayer validators.

Launch

Pitch, goal, milestones and a locked bond.

Fund

Backers pledge GEN and may withdraw until the deadline.

Prove

Creator posts a public page with the proof token.

Review

Validators read it and reach consensus.

Release

Tranche paid, or refunds plus the bond.

Questions

What network is this on?

GenLayer Studionet (chain id 61999). Your wallet is asked to add it when you connect.

Which wallets work?

Any browser wallet that supports EIP-6963 wallet discovery. Pick yours from the list when you press Connect wallet.

What if the page is unclear?

Nothing happens. No payment and no strike, and the creator can fix the page and resubmit.

Who judges delivery?

Independent GenLayer validators, using the milestone text backers read before pledging.

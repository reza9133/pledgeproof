How it works

  1. Launch

    The creator publishes the pitch, goal and milestone plan, and locks a bond.

  2. Fund

    Backers pledge until the deadline and can withdraw before it. Goal missed means full refunds.

  3. Prove

    The creator posts a public page for the current milestone with its proof token on it.

  4. Review

    Validators read the page. Confident yes pays the tranche. Confident no is a strike. Anything else changes nothing.

  5. Finish or fail

    The last approval returns the bond. Too many strikes or a missed deadline sends the unreleased money plus the bond to backers.
